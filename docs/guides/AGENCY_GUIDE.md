# Ji-Hajoor — Agency Guide

This guide is for **agencies**: service businesses that run a team of workers and want to grow by
taking Ji-Hajoor requests. (Some people call an agency an *agent provider*; in the apps it is
always **agency**.)

**Where:** [admin.jihajoor.com](https://admin.jihajoor.com). An agency administrator sees the
**My agency** pages: Requests, Team, Jobs and Earnings. Your workers use the **Ji-Hajoor Pro** app
(see the [Worker Guide](WORKER_GUIDE.md)).

---

## How agencies fit in

Ji-Hajoor reaches customers through two channels:

1. **Independent workers** get job offers directly.
2. **Agencies** cover an area with their own team. When no independent worker accepts a request in
   time, it waits in the **Requests** queue of the agencies covering that area and service, and an
   agency assigns one of its verified workers.

Your workers also receive ordinary job offers directly, like independent workers. Every job they
do, whichever way it reaches them, earns your agency its share.

## 1. Apply

1. Go to **admin.jihajoor.com → Register your agency**.
2. **Your account:** your name, email, mobile number and a password (at least 10 characters, with a
   letter and a digit). We email you a 6-digit code to confirm the address.
3. **Agency details:** agency name, contact email and phone, your base location, the **radius (km)**
   you cover and the **service categories** your team handles.
4. Tap **Submit application**. Ji-Hajoor reviews every agency before it receives requests, and
   emails you when your application is decided. If it is not approved, the reason is shown and you
   can apply again.

## 2. Your agency share

For each paid job one of your workers does:

```
What the customer pays for the service
  − Ji-Hajoor platform fee (10% in the starter pricing)
  = worker's net
      − your agency share (0–50%, agreed with Ji-Hajoor)  → your agency
      = what the worker keeps
```

Example: a ₹1,000 job with a 10% fee and a 10% agency share credits ₹100 to Ji-Hajoor, ₹90 to your
agency and ₹810 to the worker.

## 3. Build your team

1. Open **Team → Invite a worker by mobile number**.
2. The worker sees the invitation in their Ji-Hajoor Pro app, **including your agency share**, and
   joins only if they accept. Invitations waiting for an answer are listed under *Waiting for an
   answer*.
3. A worker who is new to Ji-Hajoor must complete verification (documents and background check)
   before they can take jobs.
4. **Your team now** lists each worker's availability and verification status. You can remove a
   worker from your team; a worker can also leave and work independently.

## 4. Assign requests

1. **Requests** shows jobs waiting for your agency, with how long each has waited. The colour
   changes as a request nears its timeout, so act on the oldest first.
2. Tap **Assign** and pick a worker. Workers who are verified and available now are listed first.
3. The worker gets the job in their app straight away. The queue refreshes by itself.

## 5. Follow jobs and earnings

- **Jobs** lists every request your agency assigned, with its status.
- **Earnings** shows, for every paid job, the split between Ji-Hajoor, your agency and the worker,
  and your total. Payouts of the agency share are currently arranged with Ji-Hajoor outside the
  app.

## 6. Grow with reviews

Customers rate every job, and each worker has a public profile page showing the agency name with
their rating and reviews. Good reviews mean your workers get more direct offers.

- Ask your workers to use **Share my profile** in the Ji-Hajoor Pro app with customers they meet.
- Coach workers to reply to reviews politely, especially critical ones.
- Brief your team: on time, before and after photos, explain parts before adding them, leave the
  place clean.
