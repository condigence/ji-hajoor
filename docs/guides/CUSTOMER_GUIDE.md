# Ji-Hajoor — Customer Guide

*Verified Help. Anytime. Anywhere.*

This guide shows you how to book a verified professional for jobs at home: plumbing, electrical,
cleaning, AC and appliance repair. It takes about five minutes to read.

**Where:** [jihajoor.com](https://jihajoor.com) in any browser, or the Ji-Hajoor app on Android and
iOS.

---

## 1. Create your account

1. Open Ji-Hajoor and tap **Sign in**.
2. Choose how to sign in:
   - **Mobile number:** enter your number and type in the 6-digit code we text you.
   - **Email:** enter your name, email, mobile number and a password, then the code we email you.
   - **Google:** one tap with your Google account.
3. Your password must have at least 10 characters, with at least one letter and one digit. We
   refuse passwords that are easy to guess, such as `password123`, `qwerty12345` or your own name.

> Ji-Hajoor will never ask for your password or sign-in code on a call or in a message. Keep
> them to yourself.

## 2. Book a service

1. On **Home**, pick a category (for example *Plumbing*) or search for a service.
2. Pick the job (for example *Tap / Faucet Repair*). You see a starting price and how long the job
   usually takes.
3. Tell us **where**: choose a saved address or add a new one.
4. Tell us **when**: choose a date and time. If the job can't wait, choose **Emergency** (only some
   services offer it). An emergency booking is matched first and costs more.
5. Describe the job if you like (for example "kitchen tap leaking at the base").
6. Check the **price estimate**. It lists every part of the price: base price, travel, any emergency,
   night, weekend or busy-time charge, the platform fee and taxes. If you have a **coupon**, apply it
   here.
7. Tap **Review & confirm**. You get a booking reference straight away.

## 3. While we find your professional

- Ji-Hajoor offers your job to the best-matched verified professionals near you. As soon as one
  accepts, you see their name, rating and a **See profile and reviews** link.
- If nobody nearby is free, a partner agency assigns one of its verified team. If still no one is
  available, we tell you and nothing is charged.

## 4. On the day

- **Track:** open the booking and tap **Track** to see your professional on the map as they travel.
- **Chat:** tap **Chat** to message them, for example to share the gate code. Messages stay with
  the booking, so you can look back at them later.
- **Extra parts:** if the job needs parts, the professional adds them, and you **approve the new
  total** in the app before anything changes. You never pay for something you did not approve.

## 5. Pay and get your invoice

1. When the professional marks the job complete, you see the final amount.
2. Pay by **UPI, card, net banking or wallet**. Payments are processed by Razorpay; Ji-Hajoor never
   sees or stores your card details.
3. Your **invoice** (with GST) is ready to download from the booking right after payment.

## 6. Rate your professional

Your rating helps good professionals get more work and helps other customers choose.

- After you pay, the booking shows a **Rate this job** card. Give an overall star rating; the four
  details (quality, timeliness, behaviour, price transparency) start at your overall score and you
  can change them. Add a comment if you like, up to 1,000 characters.
- Jobs waiting for your rating also appear on **Home**. If you forget, we send **one** reminder the
  next day. We never send more than one.
- Your review appears on the professional's public page **without your name**. The professional
  may reply publicly.
- Reviews from very new accounts, or ones that look unusual, are checked by our team before they
  appear.

## 7. See a professional's public page

Every verified professional has a public page showing their skills, experience, agency (if any),
star rating with a breakdown, and recent reviews with their replies. Open it from your booking, or
from a link a professional shares with you. You can book from that page with **Book a service**.

## 8. Cancel, or report a problem

- **Cancel:** open the booking and tap **Cancel**. Cancelling is free until a professional is on the
  way; after that a cancellation fee may apply, and the app shows the amount before you confirm.
- **Problem with a job:** open the booking and choose **A problem with a booking**. Describe what
  went wrong; our support team follows up and can approve a refund to your original payment method.

## 9. Your account

On **Profile** you can manage your saved addresses, change your password and sign out. **History**
lists all your bookings. **Help & support** answers common questions.

## Staying safe

- Pay only in the app. Never pay a professional in cash unless the app asks you to.
- Professionals carry a **Verified by Ji-Hajoor** badge: they passed document checks and a
  background check.
- Sign-ins and payments always use `https://jihajoor.com`. If a link looks different, don't use it.
