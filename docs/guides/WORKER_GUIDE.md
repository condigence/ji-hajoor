# Ji-Hajoor Pro — Worker Guide

*Earn More. Serve Better.*

This guide is for **workers**: plumbers, electricians, cleaners and technicians who take jobs
through Ji-Hajoor, either on their own or as part of an agency's team.

**Where:** [partner.jihajoor.com](https://partner.jihajoor.com) in any browser, or the
**Ji-Hajoor Pro** app on Android and iOS.

---

## 1. Join Ji-Hajoor Pro

1. Open Ji-Hajoor Pro and sign up with your **mobile number** (6-digit SMS code), **email** or
   **Google**.
2. Passwords need at least 10 characters, with a letter and a digit. Easy-to-guess passwords and
   ones containing your name or email are refused.
3. Complete your profile:
   - **Services & skills:** the jobs you do (for example Plumbing, AC Service) and your years of
     experience.
   - **Service area:** your base location and how far you will travel.
   - **Availability:** always available, or only during the hours you set.
   - **Name shown to customers.**

## 2. Get verified

Customers only ever see verified workers, so this step comes first.

1. Open **Verification** and upload three documents as a clear photo or PDF:
   - **Government ID** (for example Aadhaar or PAN)
   - **Address proof**
   - **Skill certificate** (training or trade certificate)
2. Send all three together. Ji-Hajoor reviews them and then runs a **background check**.
3. Your status updates in the app. Once **Verified**, you start receiving job offers, and your
   public profile page goes live.

If a document is rejected, the app tells you why. Upload a clearer copy and send it again.

## 3. Take jobs

1. On the **Dashboard**, stay available to receive offers.
2. A **Job request** shows the service, area, time, and your estimated earnings. A timer shows
   how long you have to respond. Tap **Accept** or **Decline**.
3. Offers go to several nearby workers at once; the first to accept gets the job. If you see
   "Another worker accepted this job first", nothing is lost; the next offer will come.

Emergency jobs are marked **Emergency** and pay more.

## 4. Do the job

Open the job from **Active jobs**:

1. Tap **I'm on my way**. Your location is shared with the customer while you travel.
2. Tap **I've arrived**.
3. Upload a **before photo**. This unlocks **Start job**.
4. If you need **parts**, add each item (name, quantity, unit cost). The customer approves the new
   total in their app before you continue. If you have to stop (for example to fetch a part), use
   **Pause** and give a reason.
5. Upload an **after photo**. This unlocks **Complete job**.
6. Use **Chat** at any time to message the customer.

After you complete the job, the app shows "Waiting for the customer to pay", then "Paid — your
earnings have been credited".

## 5. Earnings and payouts

- **Earnings** shows today's earnings, jobs completed and your **wallet balance**.
- Each job shows what the customer paid, the Ji-Hajoor platform fee, any agency share, and what
  you keep.
- To get paid, add your **bank account** (account holder name, account number, IFSC) in your
  profile, then tap **Request settlement**. Ji-Hajoor verifies your bank account before the first
  payout.

## 6. Reviews: your best advertisement

Customers rate every completed job on quality, timeliness, behaviour and price transparency.

- **My reviews** lists every review you received, newest first.
- Tap **Reply publicly** to thank the customer or explain a problem (up to 500 characters). You
  can edit your reply. Replies show under the review on your public page.
- Customers never see a reviewer's name, and you can't see it either; reviews are about the job.
- Your average rating helps the dispatch engine choose who gets the next offer, so good ratings
  bring more work.

**Tips for great reviews:** arrive on time, upload clear before and after photos, explain any
parts before adding them, and leave the area clean.

## 7. Share your profile and win more customers

On **My profile**, the **Share my profile** card gives you a link to your public Ji-Hajoor page
with your rating and reviews. Tap **Share** to send it on WhatsApp or SMS, or copy the link for your
visiting card or social media. Customers who open it can book you through Ji-Hajoor.

## 8. Working with an agency

You can work **independently** or as part of an **agency's** team.

- An agency invites you using your mobile number. The invitation appears on your Dashboard and
  shows the agency's name and **the share of your earnings it keeps** (between 0% and 50%).
- You join only if you **accept**. You can decline.
- In an agency, you also get jobs that the agency assigns to you.
- You can **leave** the agency at any time from the agency card and go back to working
  independently.

## 9. Your account

On **My profile** you can update your services, service area and availability, change your
password and sign out.
